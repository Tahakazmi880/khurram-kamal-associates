Questions

Before you book.

How do I book a consultation?

Fill the booking form above, email syedkhurramk44@gmail.com, or message us on WhatsApp. The firm will confirm your consultation.

What should I bring to my first consultation?

Any documents related to your matter, notices, agreements, court papers, plus your CNIC for identification.

Which courts do the advocates appear in?

The High Court, the District Courts and the Special Courts, including narcotics, ATA, banking and customs matters.

Where are the chambers?

In Saddar, Karachi, minutes from the Sindh High Court. The full address is given in the footer below.

What happens in the first consultation?

The advocate reviews your matter, explains your options, and advises you on the next steps.

What are your consultation charges?

Charges depend on the nature and complexity of the matter. Contact the firm for details before your consultation.

Do you take matters outside Karachi?

The firm is based in Karachi. Contact us to discuss whether your matter can be taken up.

How long will my matter take?

Timelines depend on the nature of the matter and the forum. Your advocate will advise you after reviewing your matter.

Is my information confidential?

Yes. Information you share with the firm is treated as confidential.

Can I consult by phone or WhatsApp?

Yes. Contact the firm by phone, WhatsApp or email to arrange a consultation.
