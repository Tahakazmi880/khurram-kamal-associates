About the Firm

The official website of Khurram Kamal Associates.

Khurram Kamal Associates (KKA), The Law Firm, was founded on 1st January 2016 in Karachi. The firm advises and represents clients across corporate compliance, financial crime, statutory interpretation and complex disputes, combining rigorous legal analysis with strategic advocacy.

Individuals and Families

Family, criminal, civil and property matters, with clear advice at every step, from the first consultation to the final hearing.

Businesses

Corporate, commercial, banking, customs and regulatory matters, compliance, contracts, disputes and representation before the courts.

Institutions

Prosecution and panel advocacy for public-sector and statutory institutions, including the National Accountability Bureau, FBR (Customs) and Karachi Port Trust.

Syed Khurram Kamal

Advocate of the High Court · Managing Partner
12+ years experience · Special Prosecutor, NAB

Syed Khurram Kamal is an Advocate of the High Court with more than 12 years of legal experience, with a practice focused on corporate and regulatory compliance, white collar crime, and complex appellate litigation. He currently serves as Special Prosecutor for the National Accountability Bureau (NAB) before the High Court in Karachi.

Previously, Mr. Kamal served as Managing Partner at Khurram Kamal Associates and Cepal and Co. He has also represented public-sector and statutory institutions as a Panel Advocate for the Federal Board of Revenue (FBR, Customs) and the Karachi Port Trust (KPT). In recognition of his professional contribution, he received the Best Prosecutor NAB Award in 2025. His litigation experience includes more than 25 reported judgments published in leading legal law reports and journals.

He holds an LL.M. from the University of Karachi and an M.Sc. in Mathematics, and is currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Law. He has also served as a Visiting Lecturer at the University of Karachi's School of Law.
