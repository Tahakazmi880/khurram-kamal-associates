About Us

Experience. Integrity. Results.

Khurram Kamal and Associates is a full-service law firm based in Pakistan, providing expert legal advice and representation in a wide range of practice areas.

We are committed to upholding the rule of law, delivering practical solutions and ensuring the best possible outcomes for our clients. With a client-focused approach, deep legal acumen and a reputation built on professionalism, we strive to be a trusted partner in every legal journey.

Founded on 1 January 2016 in Karachi, the firm advises and represents clients across corporate compliance, financial crime, statutory interpretation and complex disputes, combining rigorous legal analysis with strategic advocacy.
