Syed Khurram Kamal

Advocate of the High Court · Managing Partner
14 years experience

Syed Khurram Kamal is an Advocate of the High Court with 14 years of legal experience, with a practice focused on corporate and regulatory compliance, white collar crime, and complex appellate litigation. He also served as Special Prosecutor for the National Accountability Bureau (NAB) before the High Court in Karachi.

Mr. Kamal served as Managing Partner at Khurram Kamal Associates and Cepal and Co. He has also represented public-sector and statutory institutions as a Panel Advocate for the Federal Board of Revenue (FBR, Customs) and the Karachi Port Trust (KPT). In recognition of his professional contribution, he received the Best Prosecutor NAB Award in 2025. He has represented clients in more than 1,000 cases, and his litigation experience includes more than 25 reported judgments published in leading legal law reports and journals.

He was a topper in LL.M. from the University of Karachi, at the School of Law, University of Karachi, and holds an M.Sc. in Mathematics. He is currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Law. He has also served as a Visiting Lecturer at the University of Karachi's School of Law.
